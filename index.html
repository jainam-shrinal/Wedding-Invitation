How it began

Our Story

✦ ✦ ✦
Where It All Began

Our first meeting was the beginning of something neither of us expected. We started talking, sharing little things about our days, and before we knew it, a beautiful friendship had begun.

Best Friends, Then Something More

Somewhere between the endless conversations and getting to know each other, we became best friends. We learned to understand each other’s situations, thoughts and silences, one conversation at a time.

Late-Night Talks & Little Moments

Late-night calls became our favourite part of the day. We listened, laughed, shared our dreams and stood by each other through every little challenge.

Through Every High & Low

She stood beside me during my lowest moments and laughed the loudest in my proudest ones. We became each other’s strength, support and safest place.

And Then, We Said Yes

After friendship, understanding and countless memories, we both said yes to choosing each other and building a future together.

Two Families, One Happy Yes · 21st February

On 21st February, our parents and both families met. With warm smiles, love and blessings, both families said yes — making our happiness even more special.

Our Forever Begins

Now, as we begin this beautiful new chapter on 23rd & 24th January 2027, we would love to celebrate these moments with you.
